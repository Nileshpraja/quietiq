01 / THE CHALLENGE

The Problem

Why classroom noise matters

THE BIG PICTURE

Problem Statement

Classroom noise can make it difficult for students to concentrate and for teachers to communicate effectively. In a busy classroom, understanding patterns of sound and activity is challenging.

QuietIQ explores how local speech detection, face landmarks, and responsible activity summaries can support a supervised STEM demonstration.
